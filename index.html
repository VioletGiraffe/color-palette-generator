Slice lightness
Slices of the color space (OKLab) at the low, middle and high lightness of the selected range: angle is hue, distance from the grey centre is vividness. Equal distance is equal perceived difference in any direction; rings are 10 ΔE apart. Dots are the generated colors on the slice nearest their lightness, triangles the fixed ones; hatching is outside the selected ranges. The slider sweeps the slices through lightness.
The sRGB gamut in OKLab: lightness up, the slices' hue-vividness plane around it. The solid body is what the selected H/S/L ranges leave; the translucent shell is the rest of the gamut. Spheres are the generated colors, cones the fixed ones, rings the three slice planes. Drag to rotate, wheel to zoom.
