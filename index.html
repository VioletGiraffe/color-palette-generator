Chart lightness
Hue across, saturation up, at the chart lightness. Both axes are spaced by perceived change (OKLab arc length): the saturation axis exactly, per column; the hue axis as the average over saturations, since one shared axis cannot be exact for every row. Dots are the generated colors, triangles the fixed ones; hatching is outside the selected ranges.

3D gamut

The sRGB gamut in OKLab, lightness up. Solid body: what the selected ranges allow. Cones: fixed colors. Amber line: hardest pair. Halos reach half the Distinctness distance, so two meeting mark a pair at the limit. Drag to rotate, double-click to reset.
