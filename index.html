OKLCh range

Hue
Hue wraps when min > max (e.g. 300 → 60 covers magenta through yellow).
The selected range: hue across, lightness up. Hatched: excluded.
Chroma
Relative saturation
Min removes muted colors, max removes vivid ones. Min stricter when light: pale blues, purples and reds go first.
Lightness
Both are relative to each hue's most vivid color, which sits at lightness 50 and chroma 100, so one range suits every hue. Absolute: the same raw lightness or chroma at every hue.

Fixed colors

Six-digit hex, any separator. New colors are kept distinct from these. They do not count towards Colors and are not exported.

Avoid colors

Six-digit hex, any separator. Each bans itself and more of the same: a dull color bans duller ones, a dark one darker ones. Hatched on the charts. Right-click a palette color to add it.

Palette

Hue across, lightness up, at the most vivid chroma allowed. Hatched: excluded. Dots: generated colors. Triangles: fixed colors.
Hue across, chroma up. Lines: the most chroma at the lightest (solid) and darkest (dashed) allowed lightness. Hatched: excluded.

Names

Click a name to exclude it from generated colors; fixed colors are exempt. A color that lands in it anyway is outlined. L: the lightness where the name covers the most colors, the one shown.

Export

Click any swatch to copy its hex.

3D gamut

All displayable colors, lightness up. Solid body: the selected ranges. Cones: fixed colors. Amber line: hardest pair. Halos: half the Min distance, so two touching halos are a pair right at the limit. In the metric: reshaped so that distances look the way the generator measures them. Drag to rotate, double-click to reset.
