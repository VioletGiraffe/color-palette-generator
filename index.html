Hue
Hue wraps when min > max (e.g. 300 → 60 covers magenta through yellow).
Hue across, lightness up, each point at the most vivid chroma the ranges allow: the same chart as under the palette.
Chroma Lightness
Lightness is relative to each hue's own peak: 50 is the most vivid sRGB reaches at that hue, 0 black and 100 white. Yellow peaks near absolute L 97 and blue near 45, so an absolute range would clip the one and wash out the other.

Fixed colors

Six-digit hex, any separator. New colors are kept distinct from these. They do not count towards Colors and are not exported.

Palette

Hue across, lightness up, each point at the most vivid chroma the ranges allow: the outside of the selected region. Hatching is outside the hue or lightness range, or where the chroma minimum leaves nothing. Dots are the generated colors, triangles the fixed ones.

Names

Every name the generator can reach, each drawn at the lightness where it covers the most ground - eleven of them, white and black among them, cover nothing at all at mid lightness. Click a name to exclude it: generated colors keep out of it, fixed colors are exempt. A color that lands in it regardless is outlined.

3D gamut

The sRGB gamut in OKLab, lightness up. Solid body: what the selected ranges allow. Cones: fixed colors. Amber line: hardest pair. Halos reach half the Distinctness width, so two meeting mark a pair one noise width apart. Drag to rotate, double-click to reset.
