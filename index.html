Fixed colors

Six-digit hex, any separator. New colors are kept distinct from these. They do not count towards Colors and are not exported.

Backdrop Theme
Hue across, lightness up, each point at the most vivid chroma the ranges allow: the outside of the selected region. Hatching is outside the hue or lightness range, or where the chroma minimum leaves nothing. Dots are the generated colors, triangles the fixed ones.
Chart lightness
Hue across, chroma up, at the chart lightness. Blank where sRGB has no such color; hatching is outside the selected ranges. Dots are the generated colors, triangles the fixed ones.

Names

Every name the generator can reach, each drawn at the lightness where it covers the most ground - eleven of them, white and black among them, cover nothing at all at mid lightness. Click a name to stop the generator using it; fixed colors are exempt. Drag one name onto another to merge them: merged names generate as a single name, so two colors sharing one have to sit further apart, while each swatch keeps its own label. Drag a name out of its box to split it off.

3D gamut

The sRGB gamut in OKLab, lightness up. Solid body: what the selected ranges allow. Cones: fixed colors. Amber line: hardest pair. Halos reach half the Distinctness width, so two meeting mark a pair one noise width apart. Drag to rotate, double-click to reset.
