Identification calibration

Sets the constants in data/identify.js and the page's Distinctness default. Each palette is shown once, scattered on a ground at the swatch size; mark every pair you would mix up after living with the palette for a while, as too close or as marginal. Unmarked pairs count as fine. Palettes carry pairs of deliberately similar colors at known distances. Run the log through node data/fit.js log.json.

Setup

Axes: pairs a set distance apart along lightness, chroma or hue, for the weights. Regions: pairs in a random direction at weighted distances around the fitted boundary, one region at a time, to test whether the boundary sits at the same distance everywhere.
