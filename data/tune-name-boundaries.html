Sets SECTORS in data/hue-boost.js, the author's hue families, by where each starts in OKLCh hue. Each boundary shows its cusp color and the gamut's slice at its hue: chroma across from 10, lightness up, one scale for all, the ring at the cusp. The strip is the most vivid colors, spaced as the page's hue bar. A value past a neighbouring boundary is not applied. Served pages only: this page runs index.html's own color math, fetched from the server (.claude/launch.json, port 8734).
