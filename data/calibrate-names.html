Naming calibration

Measures how the words fall, not how the colors are told apart: each color is shown alone and you give it the one name you would reach for. Every pair of your answers then says whether two colors share a name, which is what the naming score in data/identify.js claims to predict. Rather than all 36 words every time, the words offered are those owning a survey bin within a short distance of the color, found by sweeping the grid outward in every direction - typically six of them, and the full list is one click away. Run the log through node data/fit_names.js log.json. This is a separate test from calibrate.html, with its own log; neither replaces the other.

Setup

Colors are drawn evenly through the sRGB gamut. Pairs grow with the square of the count, so 120 answers already carry about 7000 pairs - enough at every overlap the table predicts, without sampling for it. Raise it if the shortlist makes the run quick enough to want more.
