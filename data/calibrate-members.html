Palette member calibration

Which members of a palette are bad? Each trial shows one palette dealt by make_member_deal.js as the page's square of squares, in hue order row by row. Click every color you would throw out of this palette, then Done; Done with nothing marked says the palette is fine as it is. Some colors appear in two palettes, so the log can tell a color that is bad on its own from one that is bad in its company. The palettes come in random order. The log is kept in this browser between visits and can be downloaded and loaded back. Run it through node data/fit_members.js log.json.

Setup

The page's tiles are 24 px.
A round is one sitting; the next Start takes the next palettes not yet judged.
