Cell capacity calibration

How many colors a slice of the space holds. The space is cut into cells: hue sectors even in the page's hue warp, crossed with three lightness thirds in each hue's own gamut, dark, cusp and light, and three chroma thirds of the gamut's reach at the color's lightness and hue. Each trial shows one cell and one direction as a row of colors across the cell along that coordinate, evenly spaced, the other two coordinates at the cell's middle. Raise and lower the count with the keys and mark two stops: the largest count at which every neighbour is still a different color, and the largest count before any two look same-y, alike enough that you would not want both. The second is never above the first. Cells and directions are dealt in random order. The log is kept in this browser between visits and can be downloaded and loaded back, so a round can be continued. Run it through node data/fit_cells.js log.json.

Keys: Right and Up add a color, Left and Down take one away, with Shift by five. D marks the current count as the largest at which all neighbours are distinct, S as the largest at which none are same-y; marking again moves a mark. A mark at one color says the whole cell holds one. Enter accepts once both are marked; Escape clears the marks; K skips the cell, which is due again next round.
