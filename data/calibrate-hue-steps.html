Hue step calibration

A perceptual hue scale from local steps. Each trial shows an anchor hue at one OKLab lightness and chroma, with the hues below it running left and the hues above it running right, a set number of degrees per swatch; the whole run shares the anchor's lightness and chroma, so the swatches differ in hue alone. Pick on each side the first swatch that reads as a meaningfully different color from the anchor, past the point where a difference is merely detectable: a step is one minimal meaningful difference each side, and how that varies with hue is what the round measures. The two picks then sit against the anchor for a last look. Anchors are dealt on a grid in random order, so a step is never judged in the light of the one before. The log is kept in this browser between visits and can be downloaded and loaded back, so a round can be continued. Run the log through node data/fit_hue_steps.js log.json.

Setup

7.5 puts 48 anchors on the circle.
Lightness in the hue's own gamut, chroma as a share of the gamut's reach at that lightness. A side of the run ends where its hues leave the gamut at that chroma; the header says where.

Log

Anchor

Each anchor opens with both picks five swatches out. Click a swatch on each side, or use the keys: Up and Down move the right pick a swatch out and in, Left and Right the left pick; after a clear, the first press on a side takes the swatch next to the anchor. Keys 1 to 4 re-deal this anchor at 0.5, 1, 2 or 4 degrees per swatch, as does any change to the setup. On a side the gamut cut short, moving the pick out past the last swatch marks the side as clipped: the step there is beyond the run, and the log holds where the gamut ended instead of a pick. Comma and period turn the anchor a degree down or up its hue, off the grid, to gain range on a short side; the header shows the grid hue it stands for. Enter accepts, with one side clipped or with both; Escape clears the picks; S skips the anchor, the same as accepting with both sides clipped. An anchor without a step is due again next round.
