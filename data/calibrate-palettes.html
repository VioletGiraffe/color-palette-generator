Palette spread calibration

Which of two palettes is the more evenly spread? Each trial shows two palettes, A and B, dealt by make_palette_pairs.js from the same seed and count under two different hue conditions, as the page's square of squares, in hue order row by row. Judge the spread alone: are the colors evenly spaced over the range, without a crowded pair or an empty stretch. A is better, B is better, or a tie: both are OK, or neither is. Before answering, click two swatches of one palette to mark a pair that crowds, and right-click a swatch to mark a gap after it, a color missing between it and the next in hue order; the marks are optional. The pairs come in random order with random sides, so a condition is never judged by its position. The log is kept in this browser between visits and can be downloaded and loaded back. Run it through node data/fit_palettes.js log.json.
