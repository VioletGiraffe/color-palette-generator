Hue boundary calibration

Would you want both of these colors in one palette? Grade by preference, not by whether you could tell them apart: too close is no, fine is yes. Each trial shows one pair, dealt by make_boundary_deal.js: two colors a set turn apart in hue, at the lightness and chroma the deal below names, the pair placed so that its turn straddles one of the six sRGB primary and secondary hues, touches it, or stays a whole turn to one side, or swept around the whole circle at even centres; or two colors of one hue a set turn apart in cusp-relative lightness, or in chroma. Grade the pair alone. The pairs come in random order with random sides, so a condition is never judged by its position. The log is kept in this browser between visits and can be downloaded and loaded back. Run it through node data/fit_boundaries.js log.json.
