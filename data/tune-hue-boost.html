Tunes HUE_BOOST_RANGES, the page's hue ranges that change where colors sit. The closeness metric does not read them. Served pages only: this page runs index.html's own generator, fetched from the server (.claude/launch.json, port 8734).

Boost per hue range

OKLCh hue in degrees. Drag across the strip to add a range, then set it exactly below. A range runs from its start up to its end, past 360 when the end is lower; where ranges overlap their boosts multiply; outside every range the boost is 1; a range switched off is left out of your ranges. A range holds its boost up to its edges; its ramp blends the boost back to 1 across that many degrees outside them, 0 making a hard step. The strip shows the most vivid colors, each as wide as the metric's length along them, as the page's hue bar does; the curve above it is the boost, on a log scale from 1/4 to 4. The two strips below are the same colors, each as wide as its room: that length times the boost, all boosts offset alike so the total stays.
